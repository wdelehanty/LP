Stack STACK / 01

Tools, and what I actually built with each

Nothing is listed here that I have not used to ship something. Where a line names a build, the build is real and most have a page on this site.

CRM and marketing automation

  • Salesforce: the contact-role attribution model tying campaigns to influenced revenue; lead routing and speed-to-lead standards.
  • Pardot: the nurture layer under the demand engine and the lifecycle portfolio.
  • Marketo: marketing automation in earlier program work.
  • Knak and Stripo: email build tooling, evaluated and onboarded as vendors.

CDP and data

  • BlueConic: 18M+ profile segmentation, on-site dialogues, progressive profiling, lifecycle routing.
  • BigQuery: the data layer under audience and funnel reporting.
  • Bombora: intent data in enterprise partner programs.
  • Zephr: on-site experience and gating logic.

Paid media

  • LinkedIn Ads, Google Ads, Meta Ads: the acquisition layer of the demand engine.
  • GAM: owned inventory delivery and pacing for partner programs.

Analytics

  • Looker: the Performance Console and funnel reporting.
  • GA4: site behavior feeding segmentation and conversion reporting.
  • Google Optimize: landing page testing.

Content and creative

  • HTML and CSS: self-taught to ship templates, landing pages, and tracking scripts when there was no one else to ship them, including this site.
  • WordPress: site builds and landing pages.
  • Photoshop, Premiere Pro, Canva: creative production for campaigns.
  • Jebbit: quiz-format capture experiences.
  • Brandfolder: asset management.

Automation and AI

  • n8n: 52 workflows across three systems (the job search machine, Stedd, and Life OS), 36 active in production. The status rail at the top of this page counts them live.
  • Claude Code: the build tool behind Stedd, the job search machine, the event pacing app, and this site.
  • Anthropic API: transcript QA, prompt patching, and parsing inside the Stedd stack.
  • ChatGPT Enterprise: custom GPTs trained on the operating playbook standards.
  • Retell and Twilio: the voice agent with live calendar booking; a self-hosted ConversationRelay prototype sits in the stedd-voice repo.
  • MCP servers: tool integrations across the automation stacks.

Infrastructure and operations

  • Cloudflare Workers: the wardrobe API and the Stedd uptime monitor.
  • Postgres: the Stedd production database, with nightly logical backups.
  • Notion: pipeline databases for the job search, Life OS, the wardrobe system, and the achievement inventory this site is fact-checked against.
  • Smartsheet and Monday.com: stood up the Smartsheet marketing instance; leading the Monday.com migration.
  • Bizzabo: the API under the event pacing dashboard.
  • Gong, DocSend, MediaRadar, Jira: the supporting cast: call review, content tracking, competitive intel, and work management.
