Forbes Connect on the CDP

The conversion layer on an 18M+ profile CDP. It turns anonymous readers into known profiles and puts them into lifecycle and sales motion.

Problem

A media site mostly knows its audience in aggregate. Pageviews tell you what got read, not who's in market. If the business was going to monetize identity instead of pageviews, something had to turn readers into known profiles and then do something with them.

What I built

  • Launched Forbes Connect as the conversion layer on a BlueConic CDP holding 18M+ profiles.
  • Behavioral segmentation. Audiences built on what people read and do, on our own site.
  • On-site dialogues that catch a reader at the right moment and ask for the right thing.
  • Progressive profiling. One or two fields at a time instead of a twelve-field form nobody fills out.
  • Routing into Pardot and Salesforce, so a new profile lands in nurture or with a seller without anyone touching it.
  • Around it, the audience intelligence work: predictive LTV, third-party enrichment, cross-product routing.

Stack

blueconic / pardot / salesforce / ga4 / bigquery

Outcome

18M+ profiles under behavioral segmentation, feeding the lifecycle portfolio and the CRM routing. That number is the scale of the CDP, and profiles aren't leads. What it buys you is that every downstream program starts warm.

What a dialogue isAn on-site prompt targeted by behavior. Somebody three stories deep into a topic sees a different ask than a first-timer. That's the whole trick.
Why progressive profilingOne field per interaction. The profile fills in over a few visits and nobody ever sees a long form.
