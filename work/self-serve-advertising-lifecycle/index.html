Self-serve advertising lifecycle

The full-funnel lifecycle for a self-serve advertising product on DanAds: 8 programs, 29 emails, a revenue model, and the 2027 forecast now in use for vendor contract renegotiation. Built solo. Launching with the product.

Problem

A self-serve product has no account manager. Whatever onboarding, activation, and retention a human would do for a managed account has to be built into the lifecycle before launch, or the product bleeds users it never gets a chance to win back. The whole launch kit had to exist on day one.

What I built

  • Onboarding sequences that take a new advertiser from account creation to first live campaign.
  • Activation programs pushing toward the behaviors that predict a second campaign.
  • Retention and reengagement sequences for advertisers who stall or lapse.
  • In total: 8 programs and 29 emails, written, built, and wired end to end by one person.
  • The revenue model behind the lifecycle, including the 2027 forecast that is now being used in the vendor contract renegotiation.

Stack

danads / salesforce / email automation

Outcome

Built, not yet launched, and I would rather say that plainly than imply otherwise. The programs are finished and staged; they go live with the product. What already exists either way: the launch kit, the revenue model, and a forecast solid enough that it is shaping a contract negotiation.
