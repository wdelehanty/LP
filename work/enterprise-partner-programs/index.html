Enterprise partner programs

Lead generation programs architected for T-Mobile, ServiceNow, and Indeed: integrated content, research, retargeting, and delivery that lands in the partner's CRM ready to work.

Problem

An enterprise partner buying lead gen doesn't just want a list of names. They want qualified people, reached across a few channels in the right order, delivered in a shape their CRM and sellers can work the same week. Each of these had to be designed, sold through internally, and then run.

What I built

  • For T-Mobile: a two-tier product structure, so the program could serve different buyer depths at different price points.
  • For ServiceNow: an integrated partnership spanning content, research, and retargeting, anchored by a year-end insight report the rest of the program hung off of.
  • For Indeed: enterprise lead generation on the same architecture.
  • Across all three: multi-channel orchestration and CRM-ready delivery, so leads landed ready to work instead of needing cleanup first.

Stack

salesforce / gam / linkedin ads / bombora

Outcome

Three named enterprise partners ran programs built on this architecture. The revenue numbers belong to Forbes, so they stay off this site. The structure is the part I can show, and it's the part that transfers.

The flagship artifactThe ServiceNow year-end report is a pattern I keep reusing: one flagship deliverable makes every channel under it easier to sell.
