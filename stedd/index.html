Stedd

I built Stedd for trades businesses because follow-up marketing is what I've done for a living, and I know how a contractor's day actually goes.

What it is

Stedd gets trades businesses back the revenue they lose to missed calls and slow follow-up. It answers the calls that would have been missed, follows up on quotes that went quiet, turns finished jobs into reviews, and re-engages customers who lapsed. It's deliberately not a CRM, because nobody in the trades wants another system to manage. They just want to stop losing jobs.

The buyer is usually the office manager, or the spouse running the front of house while the owner is on a roof. Everything is built for that person, down to reports in plain English with receipts attached.

What's running

An AI voice agent answers the line and books jobs while the caller is still on the call, checking the real calendar live and confirming a slot before hangup. Every call gets reviewed against my checklist that night. The SMS follow-up is built and waiting on carrier approval.
