Forbes / B2B 2019 TO PRESENT

The demand engine

Built the B2B demand generation function from $0 to eight figures in marketing-influenced revenue, tracked through Salesforce contact-role attribution and conservatively counted.

Problem

Forbes monetized attention. The B2B side of the business needed pipeline: identified buyers, warmed up and routed to sellers, with revenue you could trace back to the work. When I picked this up there was no function for it. No capture infrastructure, no nurture, no routing rules, and no way to prove marketing touched a dollar.

What I built

  • The foundation first: paid media, email, landing pages, nurture, and funnel reporting, stood up as one connected system rather than separate channels.
  • Capture that I could ship without waiting on anyone: I taught myself HTML and CSS to build the templates, landing pages, and tracking scripts.
  • A contact-role attribution model in Salesforce tying campaigns to influenced revenue, chosen because it is conservative and survives scrutiny.
  • Speed-to-lead standards and routing rules, built with sales rather than handed to them.
  • A one-click outreach messaging library that sellers actually adopted, because it lived where they worked.
  • The Performance Console plus executive QBR and monthly reporting frameworks tying campaign activity to pipeline influence, account performance, funnel velocity, and channel efficiency.
  • A centralized top-of-funnel strategy and audience intelligence framework moving the business from pageview-based to identity-based monetization.

Stack

salesforce / pardot / blueconic / looker / ga4 / bigquery / linkedin ads / google ads / meta ads / gam

Outcome

Marketing-influenced revenue went from $0 to eight figures. Influenced means a campaign touched a contact holding a role on the closed opportunity in Salesforce, which is the conservative way to count it. The number does not claim marketing sourced the deals, and it is not direct-booked revenue.

Along the way, conversion rates improved roughly 2.5x through tighter capture, nurture, and routing.

On the word influencedThe number counts revenue where a campaign touched a contact with a role on the opportunity. I say influenced because that is what it measures. Generated would be a different, bigger claim, and I am not making it.
On team sizeThe lifecycle portfolio runs 10+ concurrent programs with a team of two. The scaling came from the operating playbook, which has its own case study.
