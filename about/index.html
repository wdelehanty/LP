About ABOUT / 01

A short bio, then the farm

I live in Warwick, NY. Eleven years at Forbes, where I run demand generation as an executive director; the work is documented across this site. MBA and BBA in marketing from Ithaca College. In 2026 I founded Stedd, a revenue recovery product for trades businesses, which mostly happens after two boys are asleep.

The shortest honest description of me: I like to take things apart, understand how they work, and build something more complicated than I probably needed to. That sentence covers the attribution model, the voice agent, and the chicken coop.
